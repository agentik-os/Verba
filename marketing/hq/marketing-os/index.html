Strategy

The voice agent that acts, private by design

Verba reframes the aisle: a private, on-device voice workspace for the Mac that does things, a category no incumbent occupies. Launch anchor: Product Hunt, July 28, 2026.

For power-user Mac developers who think faster than they type, Verba turns speech into clean text and action anywhere on the Mac, unlike dictation tools that only transcribe, and upload your voice.

Scale

The 365-day plan

Sequence law: win one audience, then widen. Positioning, message, one channel, conversion, measure, scaling. No scaling before the beachhead composes.

Q1 · Days 1 to 90 · Win the beachhead
  • Ship the "It's shipped" campaign, 2/day, X and LinkedIn led
  • Record the canonical JARVIS demo plus 15s cutdowns
  • Seed 10 to 20 alpha testers, collect honest quotes
  • Land r/macapps and other builder communities, manual, founder voice
  • Target 500 weekly active dictators, 3 testimonials
Q2 · Days 91 to 180 · Expand personas
  • "Private dictation for lawyers", "Jarvis for Mac" content
  • Ship /vs pages for 9 rivals, get cited by AI answers
  • Seed 10 to 20 creators, Product Hunt launch moment
  • Target 2,000 weekly actives, 5,000 EUR/mo
Q3 · Days 181 to 270 · Turn on the loops
  • Referral "free month", the leaderboard as viral surface
  • Switch on retargeting and "Wispr alternative" search ads
  • Localized SEO for EU and LatAm, Translate wedge
  • Target 5,000 weekly actives, 10,000 EUR/mo
Q4 · Days 271 to 365 · Own the category
  • Founder becomes a voice on voice-first computing
  • Co-marketing with Mac tool makers, editor and IDE partners
  • Repeatable video template, calm and premium in tone
  • Target 15,000 EUR/mo, category-defining share of voice

Guardrails (non-negotiable)

  • English public copy, no en or em dashes, benefit first
  • No priced pricing in organic, honesty as the brand
  • Exact claims: "audio never leaves your Mac", Confirm always visible
  • One executor (Marketing-machine hub), operator GO before every batch, a published post is irreversible
  • No $149 Founder line before the Go/No-Go on Jul 24 confirms the SKU is live
