Verba. Global Marketing Instructions

The single source of truth for how Verba is marketed. Every post, ad, email, video, and page follows this. If a piece of content breaks a rule here, it does not ship.

Verba is the private voice agent for your Mac. Speak it, send it clean, and when you mean it, JARVIS does it across your apps, only after you confirm. We sell the outcome, never the machinery.

1. Positioning (what we are)

The one-liner

The private voice agent for your Mac. Two promises no named competitor makes together: it is private (your voice and words stay on your Mac), and it acts (JARVIS plans, shows the step, waits for your confirm, then runs across your apps). Cloud dictation tools only transcribe and send your audio to their servers. We are a different shelf, not a cheaper one.

  • Category: join, do not create. Say "private voice agent for Mac", not "a new category of augmented productivity".
  • Beachhead first: Mac power users who live in the terminal and AI tools, then privacy pros, then voice-first operators, then multilingual writers, then long-form thinkers. One at a time.
  • Dominant benefit per persona changes; the promise does not.

2. Hard rules (never break these)

Secret tech. Never name the technology behind listening, dictation, or the AI, anywhere public: no model or vendor names, no "bring your own AI / reuse your subscription / no key" mechanic, and never the word "cloud". Say private AI models, your data stays safe on your Mac. Technical detail may live only in the Terms of Use, never in marketing.
No long dash. Zero em dash and zero en dash, anywhere: copy, captions, text on images and video. Use comma, period, colon, or parentheses. It reads as machine-written and breaks the human voice.
English only in public. All public copy is English. Internal docs may be French.
No numeric pricing in organic content. Prices belong on the pricing page and in paid ads, not in organic posts.
Exact claims only. No invented metrics, no fake testimonials, no fake scarcity. If a number is not real, it does not appear.
JARVIS shows Confirm. Every JARVIS demo shows the confirm step. It is the trust argument, never hide it.
Operator GO before publishing. A published post is irreversible. Human GO before any first-time send. One executor per campaign (no double-posting the single profile).
Always an image. Every post ships with a picture (or video) built in the real Verba design. Never text-only.

3. The reach doctrine (this is where most brands fail)

Publishing is not reach. A cold account broadcasting into zero followers gets zero reach, no matter how good the post. Reach is earned by being genuinely present in the right conversations. The rules that follow are not opinions, they come from the platform mechanics.

Do
  • Engagement first, broadcast second. Roughly 70% genuine replies into other people's fresh threads, 30% original posts. Replies are weighted far above likes.
  • Link in the bio, never in the post body. A link in the body suppresses reach for non-premium accounts.
  • End posts on a real question. Conversations get distributed, announcements do not.
  • Reddit is human and subreddit-targeted. Warm the account, comment value-first, post show-and-tell inside the right subreddit at ~90/10 value-to-promo.
  • Build in public. Demos, honest learnings, real progress. This is what every from-zero winner did.
Never
  • Broadcast changelog patch-notes. Lowest-reach format on every platform.
  • Auto-post to Reddit communities or to the profile. Profile posts reach nobody; auto-promo from a cold account gets banned.
  • Put the URL in the tweet body.
  • Chase impressions or follower counts as the goal. They are inputs, not the target.
  • Fire the same post to many places at once. Reads as spam.

5. Channels

  • X (primary, where we learn): build-in-public, reply-first engagement, demo clips. The one channel checked weekly.
  • Reddit (human, high-signal): value-first comments, subreddit-targeted show-and-tell. Never automated.
  • LinkedIn and echo networks: the value posts, repurposed.
  • Email: lifecycle and announcements, private models framing, unsubscribe respected. Never use client or partner lists.

6. Visual identity

Every visual uses the real Verba design tokens: near-black background, warm off-white accent, one live red for the recording dot, Inter type, crisp real product UI (never blurred or faked). One bright color per frame. The mic mark and verba.run sign the work. Award-level, calm, real, never AI-slop.

7. The self-improving loop

Marketing is a measured system, not a vibe. Measure each post (engagement rate, first-hour velocity, profile-visit-to-follow), run a bandit across hook, format, time, and angle, and reallocate toward winners. When something works, double down on it and clone the format. When it does not, retire it. Bounded: cap retries and escalate to the operator, never spin. The north star is voice actions actually used per active user per week, not vanity reach.

8. The funnel and the north star

Awareness to activation to retention to referral. The real bottleneck is activation, not acquisition: the first clean paste and the first confirmed JARVIS action must land in under five minutes. Do not pour traffic into a leaky funnel. The growth engine that compounds is the share loop: a one-tap clip after a confirmed action, with a Made-with-Verba end-card.
