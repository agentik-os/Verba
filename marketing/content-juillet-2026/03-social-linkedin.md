Most professionals never ask the one question that matters about the app they talk to all day:

Where does my audio go?

If you dictate contracts, patient notes, sources, or board conversations into a cloud dictation tool, the answer is usually: to someone else's server. Every word, uploaded.

For a lawyer, a clinician, a journalist, or a founder, that isn't a footnote. It's the whole risk.

We built Verba the other way around.

→ Transcription runs on-device. Your audio never leaves your Mac, and is never uploaded.
→ Sync is text-only — never the audio.
→ Local history is on by default, with an off switch and auto-prune.
→ Your API keys live in the macOS Keychain.

And because Verba can now act on what you say — more on that soon — we held the same line for the agent: even the action planning runs on your Mac, on your own AI, never our server.

Privacy isn't a setting we bolted on. It's the architecture.

Speak it. Keep it.
