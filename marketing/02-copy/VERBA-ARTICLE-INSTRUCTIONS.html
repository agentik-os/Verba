Verba. Global Article Instructions

Scope: instructions for generating ONE Verba SEO article per run (blog, comparison, how-to, use-case, privacy explainer). Follow these for every article. This is not a posting or campaign brief.

Product and positioning (context for the writer)

Verba is the private voice agent for Mac. You hold a key, speak, and your words appear clean wherever your cursor is. When you mean it, JARVIS plans the action, shows you the step, waits for your confirm, then runs it across your apps. Two promises no rival makes together: private (your voice and words stay on your Mac) and it acts (confirm-gated). Sell the outcome, never the machinery.

Hard rules (an article that breaks one is rejected)

Secret tech. Never name the technology behind listening, dictation, or the AI: no model or vendor names, no "bring your own AI / reuse your subscription / no key", and never the word "cloud". Say private AI models and your data stays on your Mac. Describe rivals as "sends your audio to their servers", never "cloud".
No long dash. Zero em dash and zero en dash anywhere in the article. Use comma, period, colon, or parentheses.
English only. Exact claims only. No invented metrics, no fake testimonials, no fabricated benchmarks. If a number is not verified, do not use it.
No numeric pricing in the body unless the article is explicitly a pricing page. Link to verba.run/pricing instead.
Every JARVIS example shows the confirm step.

Article structure (produce all of these)

  1. SEO title / H1 (<= 60 chars) containing the primary keyword, benefit-led, not clickbait.
  2. Meta description (<= 155 chars) with the keyword and a clear payoff.
  3. Slug (kebab-case, keyword-based, no tech names).
  4. Intro (2 to 3 short paragraphs): name the reader's real problem, promise the payoff, no fluff. The first sentence must earn the read.
  5. Body in scannable H2 / H3 sections, one idea each, benefit-led and concrete (JTBD, real workflows, examples). Short paragraphs, occasional lists.
  6. Comparison table when the article is a "Verba vs X" piece: honest, specific, fair to the competitor, Verba's real edge = private on your Mac + confirm-gated action.
  7. FAQ (3 to 6 Q and A, answer-first) so the article is eligible for rich results and AI answer engines.
  8. Soft CTA to verba.run (try it, raw dictation is free forever). One CTA, not a sales wall.
  9. Suggested internal links (2 to 4) to related Verba pages / articles.
  10. JSON-LD suggestion: Article + FAQPage (no tech names in any field).

SEO and GEO

  • Target one primary keyword + a couple of secondary ones; match the search intent (informational, comparison, or transactional). No keyword stuffing.
  • Write for humans first, structure for machines: clear H2s that answer a question, a direct answer in the first line of each section, a real FAQ.
  • For comparison / alternative articles, title pattern: Verba vs [Competitor]: the private Mac voice app that also acts.
  • Length: 1200 to 2000 words for pillar/how-to; 800 to 1400 for a focused comparison. Depth over padding.

Voice and tone

Confident, candid, a little witty, pro-user, never salesy, Apple-native. Concrete over abstract. Show the workflow, do not just assert benefits. Address the reader as "you".

Article types and their angle

Do not

  • Name any underlying model, vendor, or the word "cloud".
  • Use em or en dashes.
  • Invent statistics, quotes, or ratings.
  • Keyword-stuff or write thin, generic listicle filler.
  • Add posting cadence, channel plans, or campaign strategy: out of scope for an article.
