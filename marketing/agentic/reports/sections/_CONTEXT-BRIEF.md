## THE PRODUCT (solution framing)
Verba (verba.run) is a macOS menu-bar voice app. You press a key, talk, and your rambling speech comes out as clean, ready-to-send text wherever your cursor is. And now it does more than write: say an intention ("reply to this email and say the proposal is ready Friday", "create the Linear issue for this bug and tell the team") and JARVIS plans the steps on your Mac, shows you exactly what it will do, and does it across 1,000+ connected apps, but only after you confirm.
- Tagline: **"Speak it. Send it clean."**
- Live at verba.run, macOS 14+, Apple Silicon. Distributed as a .dmg.
- Live hero: "The private voice app for your Mac that doesn't just write what you say, it does it."
- Modes: Raw, Polish, Intent, Coding, Translate, Context (reads your screen). Voice Notes (talk an hour, get a clean doc). Voice Tasks. Live translation. 14 UI languages.

## THE 3 BENEFITS THAT MATTER (translate these, do not list specs)
1. **Private by default.** Your voice, and even the planning of actions, stay on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded. The benefit: dictate anything, the sensitive client note, the medical detail, the unfiled idea, with zero worry.
2. **Bring your own AI, no second bill.** It reuses the Claude Code subscription you already pay for, no API key, no markup. The benefit: stop paying twice for intelligence you already own.
3. **It does not just type, it acts (JARVIS).** Say it once, it plans, you confirm, it is done across your apps. The benefit: the follow-up you keep postponing happens in the time it took to say it.
Plus: reads your screen (Context), hour-long clean notes, live translation.
